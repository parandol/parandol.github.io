Family LineFamily tree and family schedule management app

Family Line does not require any account registration or sign-in, and the family information you enter is, in principle, stored only on your device. The Company does not collect or store your family information on any server.

1. Information We Collect and Use

  1. Information stored on your device (not transmitted to any server)
    The following information that you enter in the App is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Company’s servers or any external party.
    • Family member details: name, relationship, gender, and anniversaries such as birthdays, memorial days, and wedding anniversaries
    • Family schedules and notification settings
    • Member photos (if you add them)
    • Linked contact information: contacts (name, phone number) that you choose to link to a family member. This information is used only on your device and is not transmitted externally.
  2. Information that may be collected through third-party tools (SDKs)
    The App uses the following third-party tools to provide and improve the service and to display ads. Information may be collected and processed according to each provider’s policy.
    • Google AdMob (advertising): advertising identifier, device information, approximate location, ad-interaction data, etc. (to display and measure ads). Ads are not shown after purchasing “Remove Ads.”
    • Google Firebase Analytics (usage analytics): app usage, screen views, app version, device type, etc. (to improve the service)
    • Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash diagnostics): diagnostic information on app crashes, device state, app version, etc. (to improve stability)
    • Google Play Billing: the “Remove Ads” in-app purchase is processed by Google Play. The Company does not store payment information.

2. Purpose of App Permissions

The App uses the following permissions to provide its features. Information accessed via these permissions is processed on the device within the scope of the stated purpose.

  1. Read Contacts: to link family members with device contacts
  2. Notifications: to show reminders for birthdays, memorial days, and schedules
  3. Storage: to back up/restore data and to add member photos
  4. Biometrics: for the app lock (identity verification) feature
  5. Exact Alarm: to deliver anniversary reminders at the scheduled time

Permissions are requested only when the related feature is used, and you can change or revoke them at any time in your device settings.

3. Retention and Deletion

  1. Family information, schedules, photos, and similar data you enter are deleted from the device when you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.
  2. Backup files you create via “Export/Share” are saved to the location you choose, and you are responsible for managing and deleting those files.
  3. The retention period and deletion of information collected by third-party tools follow each provider’s policy.

4. Provision to Third Parties

The Company does not provide or sell your family information to third parties. However, the third-party tools listed in item 1.2 above process information according to their own policies, which you can review below.

  • Google (AdMob · Firebase · Play Billing) Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data in its ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

TabDeckTurns a tablet or phone into a button panel for your computer

TabDeck includes TabDeck for computers (Windows, macOS, Linux), TabDeck for Android, TabDeck for iPhone and iPad, and the web browser panel. There are no accounts or sign-in, and the Company does not collect or store your button settings or input on any server.

1. Information Stored on Your Devices (not sent to any server)

  • Button layouts, device names, the connection token and settings are stored only on your computer and your tablets or phones.
  • To switch button groups automatically, the app reads the name of the app you are using on your computer; this is sent only to devices connected on the same network.
  • If you set a button to fetch the icon of a web address, your computer requests that icon directly from that website.
  • When checking for updates, the app only downloads a file with the latest version number and sends no information about you. Copies installed from an app store are updated by that store.

2. Network Communication

Your computer and your tablets or phones communicate directly within your local network, and only devices that know the connection token can control the computer. Button presses and keystrokes do not pass through the Company’s or any third party’s servers. The Bluetooth keyboard and mouse feature on Android communicates only with devices you pair.

3. Ads and Purchases (mobile apps)

The TabDeck mobile apps installed from Google Play and the App Store show a banner ad at the top of the button panel. TabDeck for computers shows no ads.

  • Google AdMob (advertising): to display and measure ads, Google may collect an advertising or device identifier, device information, approximate location based on IP address, ad-interaction data and diagnostic data. The iOS app does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Ad consent (Google User Messaging Platform): in regions where consent is required, such as the EEA and the UK, the app shows a consent form for the use of ad data, and you can change your choice in the app settings.
  • In-app purchases: the monthly subscription, the lifetime license (one-time purchase) and “Buy the developer a coffee” (3 months without ads), all of which remove ads, are processed by Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. The Company does not receive or store payment information; the app only checks on the device whether a purchase is active.

4. Purpose of App Permissions

  1. Accessibility / input control (computer): to deliver keyboard and mouse input to the computer
  2. Camera: to scan the connection QR code shown on the computer
  3. Local Network (iOS): to connect to computers on the same Wi-Fi
  4. Bluetooth (Android): for the Bluetooth keyboard and mouse feature
  5. Notifications / foreground service (Android): to keep and show the connection status

Information obtained through these permissions is not stored or sent anywhere. You can change or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

5. Retention, Deletion and Third Parties

  1. Settings stored on your devices are deleted when you remove them in the app or uninstall the app.
  2. The Company does not provide or sell your information to third parties. Retention and deletion of information processed by the ad and payment providers follow each provider’s policy.
  3. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data in its ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
