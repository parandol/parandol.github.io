TabDeck

Your tablet as a button panel for your PC

Turn the tablet or phone you already own into a macro pad or stream deck. Shortcuts, apps and media control — one tap away.

  • Connect with one QR scan · no account
  • Buttons switch automatically with the app you use
  • Trackpad · gamepad · Bluetooth keyboard modes
  • Every feature free with unlimited buttons · Linux Wayland too

Connect in Seconds

All you need is a computer and a tablet on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. 1

    Install the desktop app

    Install the free TabDeck desktop app on your Windows, Mac or Linux computer.

  2. 2

    Scan the QR code

    Scan the QR code shown on the computer with your tablet or phone.

  3. 3

    Tap a button

    Copy, paste, take screenshots or launch apps — one tap does it.

Key Features

⌨️

Shortcut Buttons

Copy, paste, undo, screenshots and your own shortcuts on a single button.

🚀

Launch Apps & Sites

Put your favorite apps and web addresses on buttons and open them instantly.

🎚️

Dials & Media

Dials for zoom, volume or scrolling, plus playback and volume buttons.

🔗

Macros

Chain several actions into one tap. Folders and freely sized buttons too.

🔄

Per-App Switching

Photoshop, your code editor, video calls — button groups follow the app you use.

🖱️

Mouse & Gamepad

Use the whole screen as a trackpad, or hold arrow and letter keys as a gamepad.

📶

Bluetooth Keyboard & Mouse

On Android, send keys to devices like an iPad over Bluetooth — no computer app needed.

🖥️

Multiple Devices

Connect several tablets at once and switch between computers.

Button presses and keystrokes travel only between devices on your local network, never through a server. Only devices that know your connection code can control your computer. No account needed.

Update History

What changed in recent versions. The desktop app tells you when a new version is out.

  1. v0.3.262026-11-20
    • Connection: The panel reconnects as soon as you wake the tablet or phone, and notices a dead connection within 3 seconds.
    • Panel: Fixed keys staying held down while using a stick, dial or gamepad.
    • Android: Back now steps back one level while editing, and the Google Play build no longer closes right after launch (0.3.25).
    • Linux: Fixed update permission errors, the "write EPIPE" error on button presses, and the "+" key on Wayland.
  2. v0.3.252026-11-19
    • Windows: TabDeck is now on the Microsoft Store.
    • Linux: Missing packages are offered for install at startup (apt, dnf, zypper, pacman).
    • Android: Fixed a phantom Bluetooth device in the panel and the screen edges being covered on Android 15 and later.
  3. v0.3.242026-11-18
    • Linux: Fixed the next update failing to install after an in-app update.
  4. v0.3.232026-11-18
    • Linux (Wayland): Installing the deb now sets up the tools and permissions needed for keyboard and mouse input.
    • Connection window: Notes that you can control the computer right away from a web browser.
  5. v0.3.222026-11-18
    • The desktop app now updates from the latest GitHub release.
    • Windows & Linux: Fixed the app showing a default icon.
  6. v0.3.212026-11-18
    • Desktop: Store buttons and an install QR code under the connection QR code.
    • Android: A notification keeps the connection alive while connected.
