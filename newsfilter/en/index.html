Key Features

🏷️

Publisher Names

See which publisher each article comes from at a glance, and block the ones you dislike right from the list.

🚫

Block Publishers

Choose publishers — broadcast, cable, news agencies — to block, or block every publisher not on your list at once.

🔤

Blocked Words

Register keywords you’d rather not see and articles containing them are hidden automatically.

⚡

Block Breaking/Exclusive

Filter out the flood of “breaking” and “exclusive” headlines so only the essentials remain.

🔠

Font Size

Adjust list and article font size from 1 to 10 — even larger on tablets.

🎬

Video News

Watch video news on a clean screen, optimized for both phones and tablets.
