Kinship terms get confusing, and lunar birthdays move every year.

Family Line puts your father's, mother's and in-law sides into one family tree. Tap anyone to see how you are related and what to call them — even stepmothers and half-siblings in blended families.

Family dates ring as reminders at the time you choose, and everything stays on your device.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. It shows ads; a one-time "Remove ads" purchase removes them.

What happens to my data from version 1.x?

It carries over when you update. To move to another device, create a backup in Settings > Export and import it there.

Is my family data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device — back it up before changing phones.

Does it work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. You can move your data between them with a backup file.
