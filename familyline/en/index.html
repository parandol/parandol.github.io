Key Features

Untangle complex family relationships with a single app.

🌳

Family Tree

Six bloodlines — paternal, maternal and in-laws — laid out automatically by generation. Zoom to see even large families at a glance.

◎

Circle View

See kinship degrees and titles radiating around you. Switch freely between circle and tree views.

📅

Family Calendar

Solar and lunar dates with public and substitute holidays. Manage family schedules and anniversaries on the calendar.

🎂

Anniversaries

Birthdays, wedding anniversaries and memorial days on a timeline, with reminders from one day to one month ahead.

🧭

Relationship Finder

Pick two people and instantly get the kinship degree and how they address each other.

🕯️

Family Customs

Guides for coming-of-age, weddings, funerals & memorials, celebrations, folk holidays and ritual tables.

🔐

Privacy & Security

App lock with PIN and biometrics, plus PIN-protected backup and restore. Your data stays safely on your device.

🌐

Extras

Korean/English localization, contact linking, and a daily quote delivered every morning.
